# The invoice chaser — Claude reminds clients who owe you

Three parts in one weekly loop: a Bookkeeper reads who owes you (accounting app or a sheet), a Writer drafts reminders in your tone, and a Chaser runs every week, updates a dashboard and drafts follow-ups. Nothing sends on its own.

## What you need

- **Claude** — Paid plan with connectors and scheduled tasks.
- **QuickBooks, Zoho Books or a Google Sheet** — Your invoice list.
- **Gmail connector** — For drafts.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Connect your invoices

Add the QuickBooks or Zoho Books connector, or share a Google Sheet.

![Connect your invoices — Connect one](/images/guides/the-invoice-chaser-claude-chases-unpaid-invoices-weekly/step-1.svg)

### Step 2: Bookkeeper

Claude lists every unpaid invoice with client, amount, due date and days overdue. Check this list once against your own records the first time.

![Bookkeeper — List every unpaid invoice: client, amount, due date, days overdue → Unpaid](/images/guides/the-invoice-chaser-claude-chases-unpaid-invoices-weekly/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
List every unpaid invoice: client, amount, due date, days overdue.
```

### Step 3: Writer

Claude writes 3 reminder templates in your tone: gentle (1–7 days late), firm (8–30) and final (30+). Edit them once so they sound like you.

![Writer — Write reminder templates in my tone → Templates](/images/guides/the-invoice-chaser-claude-chases-unpaid-invoices-weekly/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Write reminder templates in a {tone} tone: gentle (1–7 days), firm (8–30), final (30+).
```

### Step 4: Chaser

Every Monday Claude updates the unpaid list, drafts the right reminder for each client in Gmail and updates a dashboard. Weekly runs need your computer awake with Claude open.

![Chaser — Every Monday: update the list, draft the right reminder for each in Gmail → Needs](/images/guides/the-invoice-chaser-claude-chases-unpaid-invoices-weekly/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Every Monday: update the unpaid list, draft the right reminder for each in Gmail, and update a dashboard.
```

### Step 5: Review

Read every draft before sending. Nothing is sent on its own, so you stay in control of client relationships.

![Review — Gmail drafts → You](/images/guides/the-invoice-chaser-claude-chases-unpaid-invoices-weekly/step-5.svg)

## Limits, straight talk

- Connectors it names exist in Claude's directory (Intuit QuickBooks, Zoho Books).
- Drafts-only design is the safe choice.
- Weekly runs need your computer awake with Claude open.

## Questions people ask

### Do I need accounting software?

No — a simple Google Sheet of invoices works too.

### Will Claude email my clients automatically?

No. Reminders are saved as drafts; you send them.

### Which plan?

A paid plan with connectors and scheduled tasks.
