## What you need

- **Claude** — Any plan.
- **Cloudflare account** — Free.
- **Business details** — Name, offer, photos, WhatsApp number, address.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Brief Claude like a designer

Tell Claude the business, ONE offer, ONE type of customer and ONE button (usually 'Book on WhatsApp'). Fewer choices make a site look premium and get more messages. Claude gives you the whole site as one HTML file.

![Brief Claude like a designer — the brief → Design a premium, non-generic one-page website for my business](/images/guides/premium-website-with-claude-hosting-0/step-1.svg)

**Prompt · step 1**

```text
Design a premium, non-generic one-page website for {biz}. One offer: {offer}. One audience: local women who need fast, perfect-fit stitching. One button everywhere: '{cta}' linking to https://wa.me/{phone}. Sections: hero, why us (3 points), work gallery, prices, reviews, location map link, FAQ. Distinctive fonts and colours, mobile-first. Give me one single index.html.
```

### Step 2: Add your real content

Paste the real name, address, timings, prices and photo file names. Ask Claude to keep the design and only swap the text. Never leave placeholder text like 'Lorem ipsum' on a live site.

![Add your real content — Replace placeholder text with these real details. Use these photo files → preview: Your business name](/images/guides/premium-website-with-claude-hosting-0/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
Replace placeholder text with these real details: [paste]. Use these photo files: [names]. Keep the design.
```

### Step 3: Host free

Cloudflare → Workers & Pages → Create → Pages → Upload assets → drag the folder → Deploy. You get a free pages.dev link.

![Host free — Cloudflare · Pages: Upload assets → name.pages.dev](/images/guides/premium-website-with-claude-hosting-0/step-3.svg)

### Step 4: Google Business Profile

Add the link as the website in your Google Business Profile so people on Maps find it.

![Google Business Profile — Google Business Profile: Edit profile → Found on Maps](/images/guides/premium-website-with-claude-hosting-0/step-4.svg)

### Step 5: QR code for the counter

Claude makes a printable A5 poster with a QR code to your site. Print it, stick it near the billing counter, and scan it with your own phone once to test it before printing many.

![QR code for the counter — Make a printable A5 poster with a QR code to my website → Scan to book on WhatsApp](/images/guides/premium-website-with-claude-hosting-0/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Make a printable A5 poster with a QR code to my website link and the text 'Scan to book on WhatsApp'.
```

## Limits, straight talk

- Catches: '₹0' is hosting only — a custom domain costs extra; static site only (no forms, logins, payments); skill is third-party code — read it first; demo businesses are fictional; pitching sites you built uninvited to real shops needs a light touch.

## Questions people ask

### Is hosting really ₹0?

Yes on Cloudflare Pages with a free pages.dev link. A custom domain (like yourshop.in) costs extra each year.

### Can the site take payments or bookings?

Not by itself — it's a simple one-page site. The WhatsApp button is how customers book.

### Can I build these for local shops and charge?

Yes, many people do. Ask the owner first and show them a sample — don't build sites for real shops without asking.
