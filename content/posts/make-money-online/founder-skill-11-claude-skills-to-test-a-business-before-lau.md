# Test your business idea with 11 Claude skills — before you launch

A free pack of Claude skills that pressure-tests your idea: an AI 'board' debates it, a simulated panel of 100 buyers lists objections, and skills for finance, pricing, competitors, brand, marketing and a launch plan fill in the rest.

## What you need

- **Claude** — Paste the GitHub link into Claude or install as a Claude Code plugin.
- **founder-skill pack** — Free on GitHub.
- **Your idea** — One clear sentence.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Install the pack

In Claude Code, install it as a plugin from its GitHub page (or upload the skills in Claude settings).

![Install the pack — Install the pack → 11 skills](/images/guides/founder-skill-11-claude-skills-to-test-a-business-before-lau/step-1.svg)

### Step 2: Board review

Describe your idea and who it's for in one sentence. An AI 'board' argues for and against, then votes and explains. Treat it as a brainstorm, not a verdict.

![Board review — Run the board review on my idea. Let each member argue for and against, then vote → Board vote](/images/guides/founder-skill-11-claude-skills-to-test-a-business-before-lau/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
Run the board review on my idea: {idea}, for {who}. Let each board member argue for and against, then vote and explain.
```

### Step 3: 100-buyer objections

A simulated panel of 100 buyers lists objections at your price. Simulated buyers are friendlier than real ones — use the objections to prepare, not to decide.

![100-buyer objections — Run the 100-buyer panel at my price. Rank the top 5 objections → Remember](/images/guides/founder-skill-11-claude-skills-to-test-a-business-before-lau/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Run the 100-buyer panel for {idea} at {price}. Group the objections, rank the top 5, and suggest how to answer each.
```

### Step 4: Numbers and pricing

The finance and pricing skills estimate monthly costs and how many customers you need to break even, and suggest 3 pricing options. Check the numbers with your own real costs.

![Numbers and pricing — What monthly costs and how many members do I need to break even? → Break-even](/images/guides/founder-skill-11-claude-skills-to-test-a-business-before-lau/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Use the CFO and pricing skills: what monthly costs and how many members do I need to break even at {price}? Suggest 3 pricing options.
```

### Step 5: Launch plan

Claude makes a 30-day launch plan: content, offers and what to measure each week.

![Launch plan — Create a 30-day launch plan: content, offers, what to measure → 30 days](/images/guides/founder-skill-11-claude-skills-to-test-a-business-before-lau/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Create a 30-day launch plan for {idea}: content, offers, and what to measure each week.
```

### Step 6: Test with real people

The only real test is real people. Claude writes a short waitlist page and a WhatsApp message to get your first 50 sign-ups. If people won't join a free waitlist, rethink before spending money.

![Test with real people — Write a simple waitlist page text and a WhatsApp message → First 50 sign-ups](/images/guides/founder-skill-11-claude-skills-to-test-a-business-before-lau/step-6.svg)

**Prompt · step 6**

```text
Write a simple waitlist page text and a WhatsApp message to get my first 50 sign-ups.
```

## Limits, straight talk

- Real, popular repo (~3.3k GitHub stars, MIT, Python tools run offline).
- Works as described.
- Validate with a real waitlist or pre-sale.
- Not financial/legal advice.

## Questions people ask

### Can AI tell me if my idea will work?

No. It helps you think and prepare; only real customers signing up or paying can prove it.

### Is the skill pack free?

Yes, it's free and open-source on GitHub.

### Is this financial or legal advice?

No. Talk to a professional for money and legal decisions.
