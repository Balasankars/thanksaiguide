# 5 Claude prompts for your money — budgeting, loans, saving, tax, goals

Five copy-paste prompts that turn any Claude chat into a personal money calculator: budget, loan payoff vs investing, savings goals, tax questions to ask a professional, and a wealth forecast. No bank logins — you type only the numbers you choose.

## What you need

- **Claude** — Any plan.
- **Your numbers** — Income, expenses, loans — rounded is fine.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Budget

Type your monthly income and main expenses (rounded is fine). Claude builds a simple budget, shows where your money goes in % and suggests 3 realistic cuts. You never need to share bank logins.

![Budget — My monthly income is … Expenses: … Build a simple budget → Budget](/images/guides/the-claude-finance-stack-5-money-prompts-for-your-20s/step-1.svg)

**Prompt · step 1**

```text
My monthly income is {income}. Expenses: {expenses}. Build a simple budget, show where money goes in %, and suggest 3 realistic cuts.
```

### Step 2: Loan vs invest

Enter your EMI, interest rate and months left. Claude compares paying extra, investing the difference, or a mix, with numbers and the assumptions it used. Read the assumptions — the answer changes if they change.

![Loan vs invest — I have an EMI. Compare 3 scenarios → 3 scenarios](/images/guides/the-claude-finance-stack-5-money-prompts-for-your-20s/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
I have an EMI of ₹6,000 at [rate]% for [months]. Compare 3 scenarios: pay extra, pay normally and invest the difference, or mix. Show numbers, explain assumptions.
```

### Step 3: Savings goal

Name your goal and amount (for example ₹1,00,000 for a laptop in 12 months). Claude tells you how much to save each month and gives a month-by-month plan.

![Savings goal — Goal: … How much per month, and a month-by-month plan → Savings plan](/images/guides/the-claude-finance-stack-5-money-prompts-for-your-20s/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Goal: {goal}. How much per month, and a month-by-month plan.
```

### Step 4: Tax questions

Describe your situation and Claude lists tax-saving options and questions to ask a Chartered Accountant. Tax rules change — confirm every answer with a CA before acting.

![Tax questions — What tax-saving options should I ask a CA about in India? → Ask your CA](/images/guides/the-claude-finance-stack-5-money-prompts-for-your-20s/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Based on my situation [describe], what tax-saving options should I ask a CA about in India? List questions to ask.
```

### Step 5: Wealth forecast

Claude shows what a monthly investment could grow to at different return rates in a table. These are examples, not promises — real returns go up and down.

![Wealth forecast — If I invest ₹5,000/month for 10 years at 8%, 10% and 12%, what could it grow to? → ₹5,000 / month · 10 years](/images/guides/the-claude-finance-stack-5-money-prompts-for-your-20s/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
If I invest ₹5,000/month for 10 years at 8%, 10% and 12%, what could it grow to? Table and simple explanation.
```

## Limits, straight talk

- Plain prompts, nothing to install, privacy advice is sound.
- It's a calculator, not financial advice — tax rules depend on your country, so check any tax answer with a professional.

## Questions people ask

### Is this financial advice?

No. Claude is a calculator and thinking partner here. Check big decisions and all tax answers with a professional.

### Is it safe to share my numbers?

Share only rounded numbers you're comfortable with. Never share bank passwords, card numbers or OTPs.

### Do I need a paid plan?

No — these prompts work on any Claude plan.
