# Make full cartoon episodes with Claude — only code

Create cartoon episodes where Claude writes every scene as React/SVG drawings and Remotion turns them into video. Pitch a story, design original characters, build layered scenes, change things by asking, add narration and captions, and build long episodes scene by scene.

> **తెలుగులో సారాంశం**
>
> Claude ప్రతి scene ని React/SVG drawings గా code లో రాస్తుంది, Remotion video గా మారుస్తుంది — original characters తో పూర్తి cartoon episodes, ఒక్కో scene గా.

## What you need

- **Claude Code** — Paid plan.
- **Remotion** — Free.
- **Narration** — Your voice or a TTS you can use.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Pitch and characters

Give a one-line story idea and who it's for (for example kids aged 6–10). Claude pitches 3 short stories; after you pick one, it draws the original characters as SVG with a few expressions each.

![Pitch and characters — Pitch 3 short cartoon stories based on my idea. After I choose, design the characters → Original characters](/images/guides/claude-se-poora-cartoon-sirf-code-se/step-1.svg)

**Prompt · step 1**

```text
Pitch 3 short cartoon stories based on: {story}, for {audience}. After I choose, design the original characters as SVG with a few expressions each.
```

### Step 2: Build scene 1

Claude builds the first 10-second scene in layers (sky, far background, near background) so depth looks natural, adds simple character motion and renders a preview. Check the preview before building more scenes.

![Build scene 1 — Build scene 1 in Remotion → Scene 1 layers](/images/guides/claude-se-poora-cartoon-sirf-code-se/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
Build scene 1 in Remotion: layered background (sky, far, near), characters animated with simple motion, 10 seconds. Render a preview.
```

### Step 3: Edit by asking

Ask for changes in plain words — time of day, camera zoom, weather. Because everything is code, Claude can change one thing without redrawing the whole scene.

![Edit by asking — Make it night time, zoom slowly into the auto-rickshaw, and add rain → phone showing NIGHT RAIN SLOW ZOOM](/images/guides/claude-se-poora-cartoon-sirf-code-se/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Make it night time, zoom slowly into the auto-rickshaw, and add rain.
```

### Step 4: Narration + captions

Record your narration as narration.mp3. Claude adds it and uses Whisper to make captions that match your words.

![Narration + captions — you add → Scene with sound](/images/guides/claude-se-poora-cartoon-sirf-code-se/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Add my narration narration.mp3 and Whisper captions.
```

### Step 5: Long episodes

Long episodes break if you ask for everything at once. Build one scene at a time, check each preview, then ask Claude to join them into one episode.

![Long episodes — One scene at a time → episode-1.mp4](/images/guides/claude-se-poora-cartoon-sirf-code-se/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Build scenes 2 to 6 the same way, then join everything into one episode.
```

## Questions people ask

### Do I need drawing skills?

No. Claude draws the characters and scenes in code; you just describe what you want.

### How long can an episode be?

As long as you like, built scene by scene. Start with 1 minute (about 6 scenes).

### Can I use popular cartoon characters?

No. Only use original characters — famous ones are owned by their studios.
