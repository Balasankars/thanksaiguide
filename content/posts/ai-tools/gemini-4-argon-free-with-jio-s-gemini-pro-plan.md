# Build a 3D game from one line with Gemini in Google AI Studio

Jio users can get Google's AI Pro plan free for 18 months. With it, open Google AI Studio's Build mode, pick the newest Gemini model available to you, and type one line to make a playable 3D browser game — then edit it by chatting.

## What you need

- **Jio offer** — Claim Google AI Pro in the MyJio app (eligible unlimited 5G plans, 18+).
- **Google AI Studio** — aistudio.google.com — sign in with the same Google account.
- **Laptop or phone browser** — Laptop is easier.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Claim the Jio offer

Open the MyJio app → tap the Google AI Pro banner → link your Google account. You need Jio 5G with an active unlimited plan (any age). Then check that Google AI Pro shows as active in your Google One settings.

![Claim the Jio offer — In the MyJio app → Google AI Pro](/images/guides/gemini-4-argon-free-with-jio-s-gemini-pro-plan/step-1.svg)

### Step 2: Open AI Studio Build

Go to aistudio.google.com → Build (apps). Choose the most capable Gemini model in the model list — the newest one may or may not appear for your account.

![Open AI Studio Build — aistudio.google.com: Build → Newest Gemini](/images/guides/gemini-4-argon-free-with-jio-s-gemini-pro-plan/step-2.svg)

### Step 3: Make the game

Type your game idea in one line (for example 'a cat running on rooftops collecting fish'). AI Studio writes the code and shows a playable preview in a few minutes. If it shows a blank screen, say 'the preview is blank — fix it'.

![Make the game — Make a 3D endless game playable in the browser. Arrow keys and swipe → phone showing SCORE 120 swipe to move](/images/guides/gemini-4-argon-free-with-jio-s-gemini-pro-plan/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Make a 3D endless game playable in the browser: {game}. Controls: arrow keys and swipe on mobile. Show a score and a restart button.
```

### Step 4: Improve by chatting

Ask for changes in plain words: levels, sounds, a saved high score, brighter colours. Change one or two things at a time so you can see what each change did.

![Improve by chatting — Add 3 difficulty levels, sound effects and a saved high score → After chatting](/images/guides/gemini-4-argon-free-with-jio-s-gemini-pro-plan/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Add 3 difficulty levels, sound effects and a high-score that's saved. Make the colours brighter.
```

### Step 5: Share

Use AI Studio's share/deploy option to get a link, or download the code.

![Share — AI Studio · Share: Share or deploy → Send the link](/images/guides/gemini-4-argon-free-with-jio-s-gemini-pro-plan/step-5.svg)

## Limits, straight talk

- Jio's 18-month free Google AI Pro offer is real (Google + Reliance) and is now open to all ages: any Jio 5G user on an active unlimited plan can claim it in the MyJio app. (At launch it was only for 18–25 year olds.)
- At launch Google limited Argon to trusted partners, so 'free for every Jio user' is not proven — use the newest Gemini model you see in the list.

## Questions people ask

### Do I need Jio?

No — any Google account can try AI Studio. The Jio offer just gives you Google AI Pro free for 18 months, which raises your limits.

### Can I do this on a phone?

Yes, in the phone browser, but a laptop is much easier for testing and editing the game.

### Can I sell the game?

Read Google's terms for AI Studio and check any images or sounds you add are free to use commercially before selling.
