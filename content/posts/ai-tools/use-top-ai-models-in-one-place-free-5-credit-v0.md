## What you need

- **v0.app (Free plan)** — $0. New accounts get a one-time $5 credit and 7 messages a day.
- **A Vercel account** — Free. Sign up with Google, GitHub or email — no card needed.
- **Phone or laptop browser** — Works fully in the browser, nothing to install.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Sign up on v0.app

Open v0.app on your phone or laptop and tap Sign Up. It is Vercel's AI website, so you create (or log in with) a Vercel account — Google, GitHub or email all work. No card is needed for the Free plan.

![Sign up on v0.app — v0.app: Build with AI → Vercel account](/images/guides/use-top-ai-models-in-one-place-free-5-credit-v0/step-1.svg)

### Step 2: Check your $5 credit

Open your account settings and find the billing / usage page. Under Credit Balance you should see a one-time credit of $5.00. New accounts get it once — it does not refill every month — so treat it as a free trial and spend it on things you really want to test.

![Check your $5 credit — Settings · Billing: Credit Balance → Free plan](/images/guides/use-top-ai-models-in-one-place-free-5-credit-v0/step-2.svg)

### Step 3: Pick a model

Below the chat box, tap the model name (it shows the current model, for example Claude Fable 5.2). Tap Add models to open the full list: Top picks include GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5, Kimi K3.1 Pro, GLM 5.3 Flash, Grok and Gemini 4 Carbon. Choose one to start.

![Pick a model — Claude Fable 5.2 ▾ → Top picks](/images/guides/use-top-ai-models-in-one-place-free-5-credit-v0/step-3.svg)

### Step 4: Ask your first question

Give the model your real task and who it is for. A clear prompt gets a better answer and uses less credit than five vague ones.

![Ask your first question — Task: … Who it's for: … Short points, under 200 words → One good prompt](/images/guides/use-top-ai-models-in-one-place-free-5-credit-v0/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Task: {task}
Who it is for: {who}

Give me the answer in simple English with short points. If anything is unclear, ask me one question first. Keep it under 200 words.
```

### Step 5: Compare two models

Add a second model from the same list and send the same prompt. Read both answers and keep the model that suits your work — a fast one for daily tasks, a stronger one for coding or long reasoning.

![Compare two models — Same prompt, under 150 words → Same prompt, under 150 words](/images/guides/use-top-ai-models-in-one-place-free-5-credit-v0/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
{task}

Answer in under 150 words. End with one line: how sure are you, and what would you check again?
```

### Step 6: Build a mini web page

v0 is first of all a website builder, so try it once: ask for a one-page site. It shows a live preview you can share. Free plan projects are public, so don't put private details in it.

![Build a mini web page — Build a one-page website for my brand with a WhatsApp button → preview: Your headline](/images/guides/use-top-ai-models-in-one-place-free-5-credit-v0/step-6.svg)

**Prompt · step 6**

```text
Build a simple, mobile-friendly one-page website for {biz}. Sections: a short headline about {task}, 3 benefit cards for {who}, and a big WhatsApp button. Clean modern design, no stock-photo clichés.
```

### Step 7: Make the $5 last

The bigger the model and the longer the answer, the faster your credit goes. Use fast models for simple jobs, save premium models for hard ones, keep chats short, and start a new chat for a new topic. Check the Credit Balance page now and then.

![Make the $5 last — Which model? → Save credit](/images/guides/use-top-ai-models-in-one-place-free-5-credit-v0/step-7.svg)

## Limits, straight talk

- The $5 is a one-time credit for new accounts (the page says it does not renew). Some older free accounts still get $5 a month.
- The Free plan also has a limit of 7 messages a day, even if credit is left.
- Premium models use credit much faster than small, fast ones — one long session on a top model can use up the whole $5.
- The model list changes often. Use the names you see in the picker; not every model you hear about in reels will be there.
- Free plan projects are public — don't paste passwords, client data or private documents.
- Making extra accounts to get more free credit breaks the terms and can get you blocked.
