## What you need

- **Google AI Pro** — Via the Jio offer on an eligible plan.
- **Antigravity** — Google's desktop coding app — download from Google's official site.
- **Laptop** — Windows/Mac/Linux.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Activate AI Pro

Claim the Jio offer in the MyJio app (any Jio 5G user on an active unlimited plan, any age) and confirm Google AI Pro is active on your Google account.

![Activate AI Pro — MyJio app → Google AI Pro](/images/guides/claude-opus-5-5-free-in-antigravity-via-jio-pro/step-1.svg)

### Step 2: Install Antigravity

Download it from Google's official page and sign in with the same Google account. (You don't need a second Gmail or family sharing — family members share one quota anyway.)

![Install Antigravity — antigravity.google: Download Antigravity → Installed](/images/guides/claude-opus-5-5-free-in-antigravity-via-jio-pro/step-2.svg)

### Step 3: Pick the model

In the model picker, choose Claude Opus (Thinking) if it's listed. If it's not, your plan may count as a trial — use Gemini instead.

![Pick the model — Model picker](/images/guides/claude-opus-5-5-free-in-antigravity-via-jio-pro/step-3.svg)

### Step 4: Build something

![Build something — Build my project: one page, mobile-friendly, About, Courses, Testimonials, Contact → preview: Your project](/images/guides/claude-opus-5-5-free-in-antigravity-via-jio-pro/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Build {project}: one page, mobile-friendly, sections for About, Courses, Testimonials and Contact (WhatsApp button). Explain each file you create.
```

### Step 5: Watch your quota

Quota refreshes every 5 hours with a weekly cap. Do big tasks in one go.

![Watch your quota — Your quota → So](/images/guides/claude-opus-5-5-free-in-antigravity-via-jio-pro/step-5.svg)
