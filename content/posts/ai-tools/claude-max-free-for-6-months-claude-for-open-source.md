# Claude Max free for 6 months — the Claude for Open Source program

Anthropic gives qualifying open-source maintainers and contributors 6 months of Claude Max 20x free through its Claude for Open Source program. It's approval-based — most people will NOT qualify. Check the rules first, then apply honestly.

## What you need

- **A GitHub profile** — With real open-source work.
- **Your project's numbers** — Dependents, downloads, contributors.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Read the rules

Open Anthropic's Claude for Open Source page (claude.com) and read 'Who should apply'. The main rule: a project with 5,000+ GitHub stars or 1 million+ monthly npm downloads, and active development in the last 3 months. Core contributors to big projects and people with many merged pull requests to others' projects can also apply. If none of this is you, stop here — this offer isn't for you yet.

![Read the rules — claude.com · Claude for Open Source: Who should apply → Read first](/images/guides/claude-max-free-for-6-months-claude-for-open-source/step-1.svg)

### Step 2: Collect honest numbers

Never inflate numbers — they check.

![Collect honest numbers — Help me collect honest stats for my repo → Your project](/images/guides/claude-max-free-for-6-months-claude-for-open-source/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
Help me collect honest stats for {repo}: number of dependent repos/packages, monthly downloads, contributors and stars. Tell me where to find each on GitHub/npm/PyPI.
```

### Step 3: Write your impact

![Write your impact — Write a short, honest paragraph about why my repo matters to other developers → impact.md](/images/guides/claude-max-free-for-6-months-claude-for-open-source/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Write a short, honest paragraph about why {repo} matters to other developers, using these numbers: [paste].
```

### Step 4: Apply

Fill the form on Anthropic's page and link your repo. Wait for an email.

![Apply — Anthropic · application form: Apply → Wait for an email](/images/guides/claude-max-free-for-6-months-claude-for-open-source/step-4.svg)

### Step 5: After 6 months

After 6 months the free Max plan ends — Anthropic emails you before it does. If you had a paid plan it resumes at your old plan and price unless you cancel; otherwise your account goes back to the free plan. Plan for that.

![After 6 months — What happens → Plan for month 7](/images/guides/claude-max-free-for-6-months-claude-for-open-source/step-5.svg)

## Limits, straight talk

- Who can actually get this? The main rule is a GitHub project with 5,000+ stars or 1 million+ monthly npm downloads, with active work in the last 3 months. Anthropic also considers core contributors and people with many merged pull requests to big projects.
- If you don't write open-source code, this offer is not for you — don't believe reels that call it 'free Claude Max for everyone'.
- Approval is not guaranteed and places are limited (up to 10,000 people).
- After 6 months the free plan ends. If you had a paid plan it comes back at your old price; otherwise you go back to the free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Can a normal Claude user get this?

No. It's for open-source maintainers and contributors whose work many people depend on.

### What are the main requirements?

A project with 5,000+ GitHub stars or 1 million+ monthly npm downloads and active work in the last 3 months — or being a core contributor to such projects.

### What happens after 6 months?

The free plan ends. Your old paid plan resumes, or you go back to the free plan.
