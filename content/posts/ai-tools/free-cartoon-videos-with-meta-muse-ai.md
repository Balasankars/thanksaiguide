# Free cartoon videos with Meta Muse AI — stickman, mythology and moral stories

Make full cartoon videos — stickman, mythology, moral stories or a funny desi-style character — by just telling Meta's Muse AI agent your idea. Muse writes the script, plans the shots, animates and gives you a final MP4, with no animation software and no editing skills.

## What you need

- **Muse account** — Meta's personal AI agent (app or web). Free with a weekly usage limit; paid plans above that. Officially live in the US, Canada, Japan, Singapore and Australia — not India yet.
- **A fresh email** — Sign up with a new email, not the one tied to your main Instagram or Facebook.
- **A voice tool (optional)** — Sarvam AI for natural Telugu voices, ElevenLabs for English or Hindi. Both have free tiers with limits.
- **Phone or laptop** — Everything works in a browser or the Muse app.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Get into Muse

Muse is officially available only in a few countries (US, Canada, Japan, Singapore, Australia) — India has no launch date yet. Many Indian creators sign up through a VPN set to the USA: open the Muse website or app, sign up with a **fresh email**, fill the date of birth and pick the US as country. If it asks for card verification, read the plan carefully and choose the free one. Problem: "not available in your country" → the VPN location did not apply; switch it off and on, then reload. **Using a VPN breaks Meta's terms and the account can be blocked** — read the notes below before you decide.

![Get into Muse — Muse · Sign up: Create account → Stay safe](/images/guides/free-cartoon-videos-with-meta-muse-ai/step-1.svg)

### Step 2: Redeem an invite code for bonus tokens

Tokens are your Muse usage quota. Inside Muse go to **Settings → General → Redeem Invite Code** and enter the code **77EOR5**. You get bonus tokens on top of the weekly free quota. Problem: the code is rejected → it only works within 48 hours of creating the account, and each code has a limited number of uses.

![Redeem an invite code for bonus tokens — Muse · Settings: General → Bonus tokens](/images/guides/free-cartoon-videos-with-meta-muse-ai/step-2.svg)

### Step 3: Make your first video without any skill

Start simple to see what Muse can do. Paste the prompt below with your idea. Muse writes the story, plans the shots and renders the video — you should get a playable MP4 in the chat. Problem: the video stops halfway or looks off → ask it to redo only that shot instead of starting again, which saves tokens.

![Make your first video without any skill — Make a 30-second 3D cartoon video about a clever crow… → phone showing CLEVER CROW](/images/guides/free-cartoon-videos-with-meta-muse-ai/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Make a {len}-second {style} video about {story}.
Add {lang} narration and soft background music.
First show me the script and a shot list, then render the final video as an MP4.
```

### Step 4: Add a cartoon skill for pro results

A skill is an instruction file (SKILL.md) that teaches Muse how to do one job well. Search GitHub for "cartoon video skill" or "animation skill", pick a repo with many stars, download SKILL.md (and its kit zip if it has one) and **attach them in your Muse chat**. Muse follows the skill for that chat — it is not a permanent install, so attach it again in a new chat. Problem: Muse ignores the file → say "Follow the attached SKILL.md exactly" at the start.

![Add a cartoon skill for pro results — GitHub · search: cartoon video skill → Follow the attached SKILL.md exactly](/images/guides/free-cartoon-videos-with-meta-muse-ai/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Follow the attached SKILL.md exactly.
I have no audio yet — start from ideas.
Give me 10 viral story ideas for {niche}. Each: a strong hook, suspense and a twist, {len} seconds long.
```

### Step 5: Pick an idea and get the script

Choose the idea you like and ask for the full script. You should get a short script with a hook in the first line, a twist near the end and a call to action. Problem: the script is too long for your length → ask it to cut to about 2.5 words per second.

![Pick an idea and get the script — 10 ideas → script.txt](/images/guides/free-cartoon-videos-with-meta-muse-ai/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Use idea number [your pick].
Write the final {len}-second script in {lang} (keep English tech words in English).
Short sentences. Hook in line 1, twist near the end, last line asks viewers to comment.
```

### Step 6: Make a better voice (optional)

Muse's own voice is fine, but a dedicated voice tool sounds more natural. Copy the script into **Sarvam AI** for Telugu, or **ElevenLabs** for a deep English or Hindi voice, generate and download a clean MP3. **Do not add background music** — with the skill, Muse adds music and sound effects itself. Problem: wrong pronunciation of a word → write it the way it sounds and generate again.

![Make a better voice (optional) — Voice tools → voice.mp3](/images/guides/free-cartoon-videos-with-meta-muse-ai/step-6.svg)

### Step 7: Upload voice + script and get the final MP4

Attach the MP3 and the script in the same Muse chat. Muse scans the audio, plans shots, renders frames, adds lip-sync, music and sound effects, and gives one final MP4. Rendering takes a while — wait for it. Works for Reels and Shorts, and for longer YouTube videos too. Problem: lips drift out of sync near the end → ask it to re-sync only the last scene.

![Upload voice + script and get the final MP4 — Upload to Muse → Muse render → final.mp4](/images/guides/free-cartoon-videos-with-meta-muse-ai/step-7.svg)

**Prompt · step 7**

```text
Here is my voice (MP3) and the script.
Make the final video timed exactly to this audio: plan the shots, animate the characters with lip-sync, add music and sound effects under the voice.
Format: {style}, 9:16 for Reels/Shorts (or 16:9 for YouTube). Give me one MP4.
```

## Limits, straight talk

- Muse is not officially available in India. **Signing up with a VPN breaks Meta's terms** and the account can be blocked at any time — never use your main Instagram or Facebook login, use a fresh email only.
- Card verification can start a paid plan — check which plan you pick and cancel any trial you don't want.
- Muse has no permanent skill install: attach the SKILL.md in each new chat. Only use skills from trusted repos and read the file before attaching it.
- Videos use tokens; long videos and many redo rounds finish the free quota fast. Start with 30 seconds.
- Invent your own characters — copying famous cartoon characters (from TV or other channels) can get your video removed for copyright.
- Invite-code bonus works only within 48 hours of sign-up and each code has limited uses.

## Questions people ask

Yes to start — there is a free weekly usage limit, plus bonus tokens from an invite code. Heavy use needs a paid plan.

Not officially yet. Using a VPN works for some people but breaks Meta's terms, so the account can be blocked. Use a fresh email, never your main account.

Yes. Ask for Telugu narration, or make the voice in Sarvam AI and upload the MP3 in step 7.
