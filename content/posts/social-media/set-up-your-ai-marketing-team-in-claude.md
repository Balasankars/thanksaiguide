# Never start a reel from a blank page — research-first scripting with Claude

Before writing, find reels in your niche that beat their creator's normal views (outliers), break down why they worked, then let Claude write your script using the same hook pattern — your topic, your words.

## What you need

- **Claude** — Any plan; Claude Code if using a research connector.
- **A research tool (optional)** — Tools like CreatorBerry connect to Claude via MCP and find outliers (paid credits).
- **Free way** — A list of 10 creators in your niche and their recent reels.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Find outliers

An 'outlier' is a reel that got at least 3 times its creator's normal views — proof the idea, not the follower count, worked. Free way: list 10 creators in your niche, note their usual views, and pick the reels that clearly beat it. With a research connector, Claude can do the scouting for you.

![Find outliers — Find reels in my niche from the last 30 days with at least 3× the creator's usual views → Outliers](/images/guides/set-up-your-ai-marketing-team-in-claude/step-1.svg)

**Prompt · step 1**

```text
Find reels in the {niche} niche from the last 30 days that got at least 3x the creator's usual views. List them with views, average, and the hook line.
```

### Step 2: Break them down

Paste the links or transcripts of 5 outlier reels. Claude makes a table: hook line, why it works, story order, visuals, pacing and call-to-action. Read the table yourself — you'll start to see patterns repeat.

![Break them down — For these 5 outlier reels, break down hook, structure, visuals, pacing → Why it worked](/images/guides/set-up-your-ai-marketing-team-in-claude/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
For these 5 outlier reels [links or transcripts], break down: the hook and its formula, the story structure, visuals, pacing and call-to-action. Put it in a table.
```

### Step 3: Pick a hook formula

Claude turns the winning hooks into fill-in-the-blank templates, like 'Stop doing ___ if you want ___'. A template is a pattern, not someone's words, so it's fine to reuse.

![Pick a hook formula — Give me the 5 hook formulas that worked, as templates with blanks → Hook formulas](/images/guides/set-up-your-ai-marketing-team-in-claude/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
From the breakdowns, give me the 5 hook formulas that worked, written as templates with blanks.
```

### Step 4: Write your script

Pick a hook number and give Claude your topic. Ask for your own examples and words — never copy another creator's lines. Read the script out loud once; cut any line that sounds unnatural.

![Write your script — Write a 35-second reel script using hook formula #2. My words, my examples → script.md](/images/guides/set-up-your-ai-marketing-team-in-claude/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Write a 35-second reel script about {topic} using hook formula #[n]. My words, my examples, nothing copied. One idea per line, end with a comment CTA.
```

### Step 5: Track your own

After 10 reels, paste your own numbers. Claude shows which of YOUR reels beat your average and what they have in common. Make more of those.

![Track your own — Here are my last 10 reels with views. Which beat my average and why? → My reels](/images/guides/set-up-your-ai-marketing-team-in-claude/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Here are my last 10 reels with views. Which beat my average and why? What should I repeat?
```

## Questions people ask

### Do I need a paid research tool?

No. The free way works: pick top reels yourself and paste them into Claude. A paid connector only saves time.

### Is it copying?

Not if you reuse the pattern (the hook formula) and write your own words, examples and visuals. Never copy someone's script or footage.

### How many reels should I study?

5 outliers is enough to start. Repeat every month — what works in your niche keeps changing.
