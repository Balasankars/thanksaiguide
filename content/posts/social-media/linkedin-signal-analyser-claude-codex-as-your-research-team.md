# LinkedIn research team with Claude — prospects, trends, outreach drafts

Four Claude roles — People Finder, Trend Scanner, Fit Checker and Evidence Writer — turn LinkedIn information you collect into sourced prospect lists, trend notes, fit scores and outreach drafts. Nothing is sent automatically.

## What you need

- **Claude** — Paid plan recommended.
- **LinkedIn data you collect yourself** — Profile notes, posts you copy, exports. Automated access is against LinkedIn's rules and can restrict your account.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Define your ideal customer

Describe your ideal customer in a few lines (role, company size, city, problem). Claude turns it into a clear profile with must-haves and red flags, so every later step uses the same yardstick.

![Define your ideal customer — Turn this into a clear ideal-customer profile with must-haves and red flags → ideal customer](/images/guides/linkedin-signal-analyser-claude-codex-as-your-research-team/step-1.svg)

**Prompt · step 1**

```text
Turn this into a clear ideal-customer profile with must-haves and red flags: {icp}.
```

### Step 2: People Finder (manual input)

Paste profile notes or exports you collected yourself. Claude lists who fits and shows the evidence for each — no guessing. Don't use bots or scrapers on LinkedIn; it's against LinkedIn's rules.

![People Finder (manual input) — Profiles you gathered → Who fits](/images/guides/linkedin-signal-analyser-claude-codex-as-your-research-team/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
From these profiles, list who fits, with the evidence for each.
```

### Step 3: Trend Scanner

Paste posts you copied from your prospects. Claude finds the 5 themes they're talking about this month, with quotes, so your message can mention something they actually care about.

![Trend Scanner — Posts you copied → This month they talk about](/images/guides/linkedin-signal-analyser-claude-codex-as-your-research-team/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
From these posts I copied, what are my prospects talking about this month? 5 trends with quotes.
```

### Step 4: Fit Checker

Claude scores each prospect 1–10 for fit with your offer and explains every score. Start with the 8s, 9s and 10s.

![Fit Checker — Score each prospect 1–10 for fit with my offer and explain → Fit scores](/images/guides/linkedin-signal-analyser-claude-codex-as-your-research-team/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Score each prospect 1–10 for fit with {offer} and explain.
```

### Step 5: Evidence Writer

Claude writes a short personal message for the top 5 that mentions something real from their profile. Drafts only — you read, edit and send them yourself.

![Evidence Writer — Write a short, personal outreach message for the top 5 → Drafts only](/images/guides/linkedin-signal-analyser-claude-codex-as-your-research-team/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Write a short, personal outreach message for the top 5, mentioning something real from their profile. Drafts only.
```

## Limits, straight talk

- Collect LinkedIn information yourself. LinkedIn has no official Claude connector and bans automated access, so bots or unofficial tools can get your account restricted.
- Every message stays a draft — you decide what to send.

## Questions people ask

### Can Claude log in to LinkedIn for me?

Don't do that. LinkedIn bans automated access and your account could be restricted. Collect the information yourself.

### How many messages should I send a day?

A few thoughtful messages beat many copy-paste ones. Start with 5 a day.

### Which Claude plan?

Any plan works for the prompts; a paid plan helps with longer lists.
