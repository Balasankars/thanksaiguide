# Grow your Instagram with Claude — one prompt, 5 roles

Turn Claude into a small Instagram team with approval at every step: Researcher, Writer, Editor, Poster and A/B Tester. Nothing goes live without your OK.

> **తెలుగులో సారాంశం**
>
> Claude ని మీ చిన్న Instagram టీమ్ గా మార్చండి — Researcher, Writer, Editor, Poster, A/B Tester. ప్రతి స్టెప్ లో మీ OK తర్వాతే ముందుకు వెళ్తుంది.

## What you need

- **Claude** — Any paid plan works best (Projects keep your brand brain).
- **Your Instagram insights** — Export or screenshot your own post stats.
- **Optional tools** — Automation tools like Apify/Composio cost money and scraping can break Instagram's terms.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Researcher

Export or screenshot the stats of your last 20 posts (views, likes, saves, shares) and paste them in. Claude picks your top 3 and weakest 3 and explains why. It waits for your OK before moving on — that's the 'approval at every step' idea.

![Researcher — Last 20 posts → You are my Researcher. Find my top 3 posts and why they worked](/images/guides/grow-your-instagram-with-claude-mega-prompt-5-roles/step-1.svg)

**Prompt · step 1**

```text
You are my Researcher for {handle} ({niche}). From these stats, find my top 3 posts and why they worked, and my 3 weakest and why. Wait for my OK.
```

### Step 2: Writer + brand brain

Create a Claude Project for your account and save the brand brain (audience, tone, words you use and avoid) as project knowledge. Every new chat in that Project then remembers it. Claude then drafts 10 reel ideas and 5 carousel ideas toward your goal.

![Writer + brand brain — Build a 'brand brain': audience, tone, topics, words I use and avoid → brand-brain.md](/images/guides/grow-your-instagram-with-claude-mega-prompt-5-roles/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
Build a 'brand brain' for {handle}: audience, tone, topics, words I use, words I avoid. Then draft 10 reel ideas and 5 carousel ideas toward the goal: {goal}.
```

### Step 3: Editor

Pick one idea by number. Claude writes a 30-second script, the on-screen text for each line and a caption. Change anything that doesn't sound like you before you record.

![Editor — Turn idea #3 into a 30-second script, on-screen text and a caption → idea-3.md](/images/guides/grow-your-instagram-with-claude-mega-prompt-5-roles/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Turn idea #[n] into a 30-second script, on-screen text for each line, and a caption.
```

### Step 4: Poster

Ask for a 2-week calendar. Posting yourself is free and safe; automation tools cost money and you must still approve each post. Put the calendar in your phone so you don't miss a day.

![Poster — Make a 2-week posting calendar with dates, times and ideas → Posting calendar](/images/guides/grow-your-instagram-with-claude-mega-prompt-5-roles/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Make a 2-week posting calendar with dates, times and which idea goes where.
```

### Step 5: A/B tester

Instagram's trial reels show a reel to non-followers first. Post 2–3 versions with different hooks as trial reels and keep the one that performs best.

![A/B tester — Write 3 different hooks for the same reel for trial reels → Trial reels](/images/guides/grow-your-instagram-with-claude-mega-prompt-5-roles/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Write 3 different hooks for the same reel so I can post them as trial reels and keep the winner.
```

## Limits, straight talk

- Useful, well-structured prompt with sensible approval gates.
- 'Free to run yourself' still needs paid Apify/Composio.
- Skill count is inconsistent (30 vs 14).
- Scraping Instagram with Apify can break Meta's terms — use your own analytics where possible.

## Questions people ask

### Do I need paid tools like Apify?

No. Using your own Instagram stats is free and safe. Scraping tools cost money and can break Instagram's terms.

### Which Claude plan works?

Projects (to remember your brand brain) are best on a paid plan. On the free plan, paste your brand brain at the start of each chat.

### Will this grow my account by itself?

No. It makes planning and writing faster, but growth still depends on posting regularly and learning from your numbers.
