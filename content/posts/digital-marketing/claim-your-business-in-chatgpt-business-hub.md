# Claim your business in ChatGPT — the new Google My Business for ChatGPT (Business Hub)

Put your business inside ChatGPT the way Google My Business puts it on Google Maps. Claim your free Business Hub profile, keep your details correct, see how often ChatGPT mentions you, and — if you qualify — launch your own AI agent that answers customer questions.

## What you need

- **A ChatGPT account (free works)** — Use one your business controls — ideally signed up with your business email — not a personal login you might lose.
- **Your business website** — Claiming needs a website. Online businesses need one to add a new listing.
- **Business phone, email or domain access** — To prove the business is yours (or a Shopify store login).
- **ID and business papers** — Government ID, business registration details and proof you can act for the business — for the Persona identity check.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Know what it is and check if you can use it

Businesses in ChatGPT went into beta on 10 November 2026. It has three parts inside Business Hub: a business profile you can claim and edit, Insights (how often ChatGPT mentions you), and Business Agents (your own AI chat for customers). Right now online businesses with a website can claim or add a listing, but local shops and restaurants can only claim a listing that already exists in the US — so many Indian local businesses will see no listing yet. If that's you, keep your website and Google profile strong and come back as OpenAI expands it.

![Know what it is and check if you can use it — Business Hub (beta) → Today](/images/guides/claim-your-business-in-chatgpt-business-hub/step-1.svg)

**Prompt · step 1**

```text
I run {business} in {city}. We sell {offer} to {customers}. Our website is {website}.

OpenAI's Businesses in ChatGPT (Business Hub) is in beta: online businesses with a website can claim or add a listing, local stores can only claim existing US listings, and Business Agents are only for eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses.

In simple words, tell me: 1) Is my business an online business, a local business, or both? 2) What can I probably do in Business Hub today? 3) If I can't claim yet, what 5 things should I fix on my website now so ChatGPT describes us correctly?
```

### Step 2: Keep everything ready before you start

Decide which ChatGPT account will own the profile — a shared business account is better than one person's login. Keep your business phone, business email (on your own domain if possible) and website login handy. For the identity check you'll need a government ID, your business registration details and proof that you can represent the business. Make sure your business name, address and phone match exactly on your website, papers and profile — mismatches are the most common reason a claim gets stuck.

![Keep everything ready before you start — Keep ready → Must match](/images/guides/claim-your-business-in-chatgpt-business-hub/step-2.svg)

### Step 3: Open Business Hub and find your business

Open ChatGPT on the web and tap Business Hub in the left sidebar (or go to chatgpt.com/business-hub). Tap Add, search for your business by name and accept the Businesses in ChatGPT terms. Pick the listing that matches and confirm the ChatGPT account that will manage it. If you see Contact support instead, the listing is locked — use that button. If someone else already claimed your business, use Dispute ownership.

![Open Business Hub and find your business — chatgpt.com/business-hub: Business Hub → Contact support](/images/guides/claim-your-business-in-chatgpt-business-hub/step-3.svg)

### Step 4: Prove the business is yours

Choose one verification method: a text or automated call to your business phone, a code sent to your business email, a TXT record added to your website's DNS, or connecting your Shopify store. Then wait for OpenAI to approve the claim — there is no fixed time, and you can cancel while it's under review. DNS changes can take a few hours to show up, so if verification fails right away, wait and try again.

![Prove the business is yours — Verify with one → Under review](/images/guides/claim-your-business-in-chatgpt-business-hub/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
I need to add a TXT record to my domain {website} to verify my business in ChatGPT Business Hub. I bought the domain from [GoDaddy / Hostinger / Cloudflare / other — I'll tell you].

Give me click-by-click steps for that provider: where to find DNS settings, what to put in Type, Name/Host, Value and TTL, and how to check the record is live. Keep it simple — I'm not technical.
```

### Step 5: Pass the ID check and fill your profile

Claiming alone is not enough: finish the Persona identity check from your business overview. Approval unlocks publishing, product feeds, Insights and the verified badge. Then fill the profile tabs — About, Contact, Photos, Delivery and Returns — and watch the live preview. Write the About text as plain, specific facts (what you sell, for whom, where), not ad slogans, and keep delivery and returns the same as on your website. Note: once published, a profile can't be deleted by OpenAI support, so check it carefully.

![Pass the ID check and fill your profile — Persona ID check → About](/images/guides/claim-your-business-in-chatgpt-business-hub/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Write the About description for my ChatGPT business profile.

Business: {business}
Location / service area: {city}
What we sell: {offer}
Customers: {customers}
Website: {website}

Rules: 60–90 words, plain facts only (what, for whom, where, hours if I give them), no hype words like 'best' or 'No.1', no claims I haven't given you. Then list 5 questions customers might ask ChatGPT about us that this description should answer.
```

### Step 6: Read your Insights the right way

Once there is enough data, Insights shows Mentions, Website clicks and up to five discovery topics, for the last 7 or 28 days. For online businesses a mention counts only when ChatGPT points to your website — just saying your brand name doesn't count. A blank number can mean 'not enough data yet', not zero. Insights doesn't show prompts, chats, competitors or other AI apps, so keep your own simple monthly log to see the trend.

![Read your Insights the right way — Insights · 28 days → Explain my numbers](/images/guides/claim-your-business-in-chatgpt-business-hub/step-6.svg)

**Prompt · step 6**

```text
Here are my ChatGPT Business Hub Insights for {business}: Mentions = …, Website clicks = …, top discovery topics = … (last 28 days). Last month: … 

Explain in simple words what changed and why it might have changed. Then give me 3 small actions for my website {website} this month that could help ChatGPT mention and link us more for {offer}.
```

### Step 7: Set up your AI Business Agent (if you qualify)

A Business Agent is a labelled AI chat that represents your business inside ChatGPT. It answers questions from the information you give it, follows your answer rules and links people to your website. Today only eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses can build one; if Agent builder says it isn't available yet, you're not eligible in your region. The agent can't complete purchases, bookings or order lookups, must say it's AI, and can't pressure people — so the sale still happens on your website. To set it up: review its information, write guidelines, test with real customer questions, then publish.

![Set up your AI Business Agent (if you qualify) — Is it open on Sunday? → Before publish](/images/guides/claim-your-business-in-chatgpt-business-hub/step-7.svg)

**Prompt · step 7**

```text
Write answer guidelines for my ChatGPT Business Agent for {business} ({offer}, website {website}).

Include: tone (friendly, simple English), what it must always do (give correct facts, send people to {website} to order or pay), what it must never do (promise discounts I didn't list, give medical/legal advice, ask for personal data), and how to answer when it doesn't know.

Then give me 10 real customer test questions to check the agent before I publish it.
```

## Limits, straight talk

- It's a beta. Local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing US listing; eligible online businesses may add a new one. Business Agents are only for eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses so far — OpenAI says more markets and categories will come later.
- Claiming is free and needs no ad spend, but it does not guarantee more visibility or let you control what ChatGPT recommends. Your website, reviews and other sites still shape what it says.
- The Business Agent can't take payments, bookings or order lookups — it answers questions and links to your website. Promoting your agent through ChatGPT Ads is paid.
- Insights covers ChatGPT only, in total numbers. You won't see people's chats, prompts or competitor data, and blanks don't mean zero.
- The identity check (Persona) may ask for a government ID and business papers. Make sure names and addresses match your official records before you start.
- Each location and each brand is claimed separately — claiming the main brand doesn't claim its branches.

## Questions people ask

### Does it cost anything?

Claiming, managing your profile and seeing Insights are free in the beta. Only promoting a Business Agent through ChatGPT Ads is paid.

### Can my shop in India claim it now?

Local stores can only claim existing US listings for now. If you sell online and have a website, you may be able to add a listing — check Business Hub.

### Can I do it on my phone?

Business Hub is on ChatGPT's web version. A laptop is easier, especially if you verify with a DNS record.
