# SEO + AI-search (GEO) autopilot with Claude

Claude runs a consultant's loop — audit, diagnose, plan, fix, re-audit — for both Google and AI search tools. Connect Search Console, check whether AI assistants mention you, add robots.txt and schema, and track it on a dashboard.

## What you need

- **Claude Code** — Paid plan.
- **Search Console MCP** — Free.
- **Optional rank data** — Pay-per-use.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Audit

Claude audits your site for both Google and AI search: clear content, schema, robots.txt, speed and FAQs.

![Audit — Audit my site for SEO and AI-search readiness → Audit covers](/images/guides/the-seo-geo-autopilot-full-setup-guide/step-1.svg)

**Prompt · step 1**

```text
Audit {site} for SEO and AI-search readiness: content clarity, schema, robots.txt, page speed, FAQs.
```

### Step 2: AI visibility check

Claude writes 10 questions customers ask AI tools and shows you how to check if your site is mentioned in the answers. Note the results in a table every month.

![AI visibility check — Write 10 questions customers ask AI tools. How do I check if I'm mentioned? → AI visibility](/images/guides/the-seo-geo-autopilot-full-setup-guide/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
Write 10 questions customers ask AI tools, like '{q}'. Tell me how to check if my site is mentioned, and track it monthly.
```

### Step 3: Diagnose and plan

Claude picks the top 5 problems and puts the fixes in order — biggest impact first.

![Diagnose and plan — Diagnose the top 5 problems and make a fix plan in priority order → Fix plan](/images/guides/the-seo-geo-autopilot-full-setup-guide/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Diagnose the top 5 problems and make a fix plan in priority order.
```

### Step 4: Fix

Claude adds schema (Organization, Course, FAQ) and fixes robots.txt so search and AI crawlers can read your pages. llms.txt is only a proposal and Google doesn't use it — clear content and schema matter more.

![Fix — Add proper schema and fix robots.txt. Show me the changes → What matters](/images/guides/the-seo-geo-autopilot-full-setup-guide/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Add proper schema (Organization, Course, FAQ) and fix robots.txt. Show me the changes.
```

### Step 5: Re-audit and dashboard

After fixes, Claude re-audits and builds a simple dashboard of rankings and AI mentions so you can see progress month by month.

![Re-audit and dashboard — Re-audit after fixes and build a simple dashboard → Dashboard](/images/guides/the-seo-geo-autopilot-full-setup-guide/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Re-audit after fixes and build a simple dashboard of rankings and AI mentions.
```

## Limits, straight talk

- Workflow and tools are real; rank data tools are optional and pay-per-use.
- llms.txt is only a proposal and Google doesn't use it — schema and clear answers matter more.
- No method can guarantee AI tools will mention you; track it as measurement.

## Questions people ask

### What is GEO?

Generative engine optimisation — helping AI search tools find, understand and mention your site.

### Do I need paid rank data?

No. Search Console is free; paid rank data is optional.

### Can anyone guarantee AI will mention me?

No. You can only make it more likely and measure the results.
