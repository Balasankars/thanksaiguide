# 5 viral AI ad formats for e-commerce — prompts with Claude

Five ad formats that stop the scroll — claymation, paper animation, a song-style ad, a talking product, and a 'what happens if…' timeline. Claude writes the image and video prompts; you generate them in your AI image/video tool, keeping your real product photo in every shot.

## What you need

- **Claude** — Any plan — writes the prompts.
- **An AI image tool** — To make the stills.
- **An AI image-to-video tool** — To animate each still (usually paid credits).
- **Your product photo** — Clear, plain background.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Claymation ad

Attach one clear photo of your product on a plain background, then send the prompt. Claude returns 6 numbered image prompts that tell a tiny story (problem → product → happy result). Generate each still in your image tool with the product photo attached. If the product looks different in a still, say "keep the bottle exactly like the photo — same label, same colour" and regenerate only that one.

![Claymation ad — Write 6 image prompts for a claymation-style ad for my product → Claymation ad](/images/guides/5-ai-ad-formats-a-claude-skills-pack/step-1.svg)

**Prompt · step 1**

```text
Write 6 image prompts for a claymation-style ad for {product} by {brand}. Plasticine textures, warm studio light, the real product shown exactly like my attached photo in every shot. Story: problem → product → result ({benefit}). Then write a short animation prompt for each image.
```

### Step 2: Paper animation ad

Same idea, new look: layered paper cut-outs with soft shadows. Paper style hides small AI mistakes, so it's a good first format if your image tool struggles with clay textures. Keep all 6 stills the same size (9:16) so they cut together smoothly.

![Paper animation ad — Write 6 image prompts for a cut-paper animation ad → Paper animation ad](/images/guides/5-ai-ad-formats-a-claude-skills-pack/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
Write 6 image prompts for a cut-paper animation ad for {product}: layered paper, soft shadows, pieces sliding into place. Include my product exactly as in the photo.
```

### Step 3: Talking product ad

Claude writes a 20-second script where your product talks, plus one image prompt and one short animation prompt per line. Make the face simple (two eyes, one mouth) so the product stays recognisable. Record the lines in your own voice or a voice tool, then line them up with each clip.

![Talking product ad — Write a 20-second script where my product has a cute face and talks → phone showing HI, I'M YOUR PRODUCT](/images/guides/5-ai-ad-formats-a-claude-skills-pack/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Write a 20-second script where {product} has a cute face and talks directly to the viewer about {benefit}, then image and animation prompts for each line.
```

### Step 4: Song-style ad

Ask only for original lyrics — never use a famous song's tune or words in an ad. Claude gives you a 15-second jingle and shot prompts of a person singing it with the product. Keep the brand name in the first and last line so people remember it.

![Song-style ad — Write a catchy 15-second original jingle about my brand → Song-style ad](/images/guides/5-ai-ad-formats-a-claude-skills-pack/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Write a catchy 15-second original jingle about {brand} and {benefit} (original lyrics only), plus shot prompts of a person singing it to camera with the product.
```

### Step 5: 'What happens if' timeline

A day 1 → week 1 → day 30 timeline is powerful, but only show results you can honestly back up. Never promise health or medical results (for example 'cures hair fall'). Claude gives one image prompt for each point on the timeline; add the date as big text on each clip.

!['What happens if' timeline — Write a 'What happens if you use it every day' timeline → 'What happens if…'](/images/guides/5-ai-ad-formats-a-claude-skills-pack/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Write a 'What happens if you use {product} every day' timeline: day 1, week 1, day 30 — realistic, no medical promises. Image prompt for each step.
```

## Questions people ask

### Do I need a paid AI tool?

Claude can write all the prompts on its free plan. Turning stills into moving video usually needs credits in an image-to-video tool, so expect a small cost for the animation part.

### Can I make these ads in Telugu?

Yes — add 'Write the script and on-screen text in Telugu' to any prompt. Keep the brand name in English letters if that's how customers search for it.

### Which format should I try first?

Start with the paper animation ad: it's the easiest to keep consistent and works well even when the image tool isn't perfect.
