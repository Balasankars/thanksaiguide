# Get ChatGPT and AI assistants to recommend your business

People now ask AI assistants 'which is the best…?'. Answer-engine optimisation (AEO) helps your business show up: find the real questions buyers ask, rewrite your pages to answer them clearly, check your site's technical health, list your products/services where AI tools read, collect real reviews, and track mentions every week.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Find the real questions

Claude lists 20 real questions people in your city ask before choosing your kind of service, grouped by price, quality, location and comparison. These are the questions AI assistants try to answer.

![Find the real questions — List 20 questions people in my city ask AI assistants before choosing my service → Questions](/images/guides/the-aeo-guide-get-chatgpt-to-recommend-you/step-1.svg)

**Prompt · step 1**

```text
List 20 questions people in {city} ask AI assistants or Google before choosing {service}. Group them: price, quality, location, comparison.
```

### Step 2: Rewrite your pages

Rewrite your homepage and services page so each question gets a short, direct answer with prices, location and proof. Keep your own voice — just make the answers easy to find.

![Rewrite your pages — Rewrite my homepage and services page so they answer those questions directly → your-site.com/services: A question as the heading](/images/guides/the-aeo-guide-get-chatgpt-to-recommend-you/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
Rewrite my homepage and services page for {biz} so they answer those questions directly: clear headings, short answers, prices, location, proof. Keep my voice.
```

### Step 3: Technical check

Claude checks speed, mobile view, structured data (schema), whether robots.txt lets AI crawlers in, and clear contact details, then lists fixes in order.

![Technical check — Check my site: page speed, mobile, schema, robots.txt allowing AI crawlers → Technical check](/images/guides/the-aeo-guide-get-chatgpt-to-recommend-you/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Check my site for: page speed, mobile, schema (LocalBusiness, Course, FAQ), robots.txt allowing AI crawlers, and clear contact info. List fixes.
```

### Step 4: Get listed and reviewed

Get listed where AI tools read (Google Business Profile and trusted directories) and ask happy customers for honest reviews. Never write fake reviews — they can get your listing removed.

![Get listed and reviewed — List where I should be listed and write a WhatsApp message asking for reviews → Be findable](/images/guides/the-aeo-guide-get-chatgpt-to-recommend-you/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
List where I should be listed (Google Business Profile, directories) and write a WhatsApp message asking happy students for honest reviews.
```

### Step 5: Track weekly

Every Monday, ask 5 'best ___ in ___' questions to AI assistants and note if your business is mentioned. No method can guarantee AI recommends you — this table just shows whether things improve.

![Track weekly — Every Monday → Honest note](/images/guides/the-aeo-guide-get-chatgpt-to-recommend-you/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Every Monday, ask 5 AI-style questions like 'best {service} in {city}' and record whether {biz} is mentioned. Keep a simple table.
```

## Limits, straight talk

## Questions people ask

### What is AEO?

Answer-engine optimisation: making your business easy for AI assistants like ChatGPT to find, understand and recommend.

### Is it different from SEO?

It builds on SEO. The extra focus is on clear answers to real questions and being listed where AI tools read.

### How long does it take?

Changes can take weeks to show. Track weekly and be patient.
