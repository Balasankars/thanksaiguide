# Build your AI prospecting team — research to email drafts

Four AI roles — Buyer Investigator, Prospect Researcher, Outreach Writer, Sales Analyst — share context, test messages on different audiences, and save emails as Gmail drafts. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

## What you need

- **Claude** — Paid plan with Gmail connector.
- **A prospect list** — Businesses you're allowed to contact.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Buyer Investigator

Claude describes your ideal buyer: who decides, their problems, what they've already tried and their objections. Correct anything that doesn't match real customers you've met.

![Buyer Investigator — Describe the ideal buyer for my offer in my city → ideal buyer](/images/guides/build-your-ai-prospecting-team-4-skills-19-prompts/step-1.svg)

**Prompt · step 1**

```text
Describe the ideal buyer for {offer} in {city}: who decides, their problems, what they've tried, objections.
```

### Step 2: Prospect Researcher

Give 10 businesses you're allowed to contact. Claude checks what's missing online (no website, few reviews) and shows the evidence for each.

![Prospect Researcher — Research these 10 businesses: what's missing online, with evidence → 10 businesses](/images/guides/build-your-ai-prospecting-team-4-skills-19-prompts/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
Research these 10 businesses [list]: what's missing online (no website, few reviews), with evidence.
```

### Step 3: Outreach Writer

Claude writes a short personal email for each, mentioning their specific gap, with your real name, business and an opt-out line — then saves them as Gmail drafts. Nothing is sent automatically.

![Outreach Writer — Write a short personal email for each, mentioning their specific gap → Every email has](/images/guides/build-your-ai-prospecting-team-4-skills-19-prompts/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Write a short personal email for each, mentioning their specific gap. Include my name, business and an opt-out line. Save as Gmail drafts.
```

### Step 4: A/B test

Write two versions for two different audiences (for example dentists vs physiotherapists) and see which gets more replies.

![A/B test — Write 2 versions for two audiences so I can compare replies → A/B test](/images/guides/build-your-ai-prospecting-team-4-skills-19-prompts/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Write 2 versions for two audiences (dentists vs physiotherapists) so I can compare replies.
```

### Step 5: Sales Analyst

Paste your reply results. Claude says what worked and what to change for the next batch.

![Sales Analyst — Reply results → What worked and what to change?](/images/guides/build-your-ai-prospecting-team-4-skills-19-prompts/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Here are reply results: [paste]. What worked and what to change?
```

## Limits, straight talk

- Codex-based; source code is on GitHub.
- Outreach stays as drafts.
- Cold email still has to follow anti-spam rules (opt-out, real identity).

## Questions people ask

### Is cold email legal?

Generally yes if you use your real identity, write to businesses, and include a clear way to opt out. Never buy email lists.

### Does Claude send the emails?

No. Emails are saved as drafts; you review and send each one.

### Which plan do I need?

A paid Claude plan with the Gmail connector.
