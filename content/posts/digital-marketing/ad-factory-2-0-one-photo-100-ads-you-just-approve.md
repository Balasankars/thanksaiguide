# Ad factory — one product photo to dozens of ads with Claude

Claude writes dozens of hooks and full ad scripts, builds motion ads in code for free on your laptop, and (optionally) uses a paid image/video AI for visual ads. A results check every few days tells you which ads to stop. Nothing spends money without your approval.

## What you need

- **Claude Code** — Paid plan.
- **HyperFrames** — Free — motion ads.
- **Image/video AI (optional)** — Paid credits.
- **Ads account connector (optional)** — To read results.

## Step by step

### Step 1: Hooks

Describe your product, your buyer and their pain. Claude writes 30 hooks in different styles and ranks the top 10. Only use bold claims you can prove.

![Hooks — Write 30 ad hooks for my product, for my buyer, whose pain is… → Hook types](/images/guides/ad-factory-2-0-one-photo-100-ads-you-just-approve/step-1.svg)

**Prompt · step 1**

```text
Write 30 ad hooks for {product}, for {buyer}, whose pain is: {pain}. Mix: question, bold claim (true only), story, number, 'stop doing X'. Rank the top 10.
```

### Step 2: Full ads

Claude turns the top 10 hooks into full 15-second scripts: hook, problem, solution, proof, offer, call-to-action.

![Full ads — Turn the top 10 hooks into 15-second ad scripts → Each script · 15 s](/images/guides/ad-factory-2-0-one-photo-100-ads-you-just-approve/step-2.svg)

**Prompt · step 2**

```text
Turn the top 10 hooks into 15-second ad scripts: hook, problem, solution, proof, offer, CTA.
```

### Step 3: Motion ads (free)

Claude builds 5 motion ads in HyperFrames using your product photo and renders them on your laptop — free, no credits needed.

![Motion ads (free) — product.png → 5 motion ads · 1080×1920](/images/guides/ad-factory-2-0-one-photo-100-ads-you-just-approve/step-3.svg)

**Prompt · step 3**

```text
Build 5 of these as motion-graphics ads in HyperFrames, 1080x1920, using my product photo product.png. Render all.
```

### Step 4: Image ads (optional)

If you want picture ads, Claude writes image prompts and you generate them yourself in a paid image tool. Nothing spends money without your OK.

![Image ads (optional) — Write image prompts for 5 static ads using my product photo — I'll generate them myself → Your choice](/images/guides/ad-factory-2-0-one-photo-100-ads-you-just-approve/step-4.svg)

**Prompt · step 4**

```text
Write image prompts for 5 static ads using my product photo — I'll generate them myself.
```

### Step 5: Read results

After a few days, paste your real ad results (or connect your ads account). Claude says which ads to stop, which to scale and what new hooks to test next.

![Read results — Ad results → Which ads to stop, which to scale, what new hooks to test?](/images/guides/ad-factory-2-0-one-photo-100-ads-you-just-approve/step-5.svg)

**Prompt · step 5**

```text
Here are the ad results [paste or connector]. Which ads to stop, which to scale, and what new hooks to test?
```

## Limits, straight talk

- Motion ads built in code are free; image and video AI ads cost credits in those tools.
- Reading ad results needs your own ads account data — paste it or connect your account.
- Only make claims about your product that you can prove.

## Questions people ask

### Do I need to spend money on AI images?

No. The motion ads in step 3 are made in code for free. Image ads are optional.

### How many ads should I run at once?

Start with 3–5 so each gets enough views to judge. Add new ones as you stop the weak ones.

### Can Claude run my ads for me?

No. Claude writes and analyses; you approve and publish ads in your ads account.
